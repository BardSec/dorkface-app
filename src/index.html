What has Google found on your site?

Google indexes far more than web pages: spreadsheets, PDFs, backups, login screens and open folders. dorkface builds the search operators (site:, filetype:, intitle:…) that find them, so you can audit your own organization without memorizing the syntax.

Use it on your own organization

These searches show what anyone can already find, but that doesn't make it fair game. Search domains you're responsible for, or have permission to test.

  • Don't download, keep or share personal data you come across on someone else's site. Tell the owner instead.
  • Google's results lag behind: a file may already be gone, or still cached after you delete it.
  • No results doesn't mean nothing is exposed. Google only shows what it has indexed.

Found something?

  1. Remove the file, or put it behind a sign-in. If it holds passwords or keys, change them first.
  2. Check whether it held personal data about customers, employees or anyone else. If so, follow your organization's incident process; some laws require telling the people affected.
  3. Ask Google to drop it: in Google Search Console → Removals for a site you own, or with Google's Refresh outdated content tool for one you don't.
  4. Fix the cause: turn off directory listings, and block .env, .bak and .sql files on the web server.
